# Off-Plan Dispositions get their own table, not a discriminator on the Plan

The Review phase surfaces the union of Plan members and off-Plan engaged Outcomes (CONTEXT.md § Engagement), and each non-completed Outcome in that surface takes a Disposition (`rollover | leave | maybe`). Plan-member Dispositions have always persisted to `focus_session_tasks.disposition`. But an off-Plan engaged Outcome has no `focus_session_tasks` row — the Plan is fixed at Planning and never auto-grows from engagement (ADR-0002) — so its Disposition had nowhere durable to land: a `rollover` was silently dropped and the "worked on in a session" stamp missed it (issue #418). We give off-Plan Dispositions durable storage in a **new synced table `focus_session_dispositions`**, keyed `(focus_session_id, task_id)`, and UNION the two homes in the query helpers (`getLastClosedSessionRolloverTaskIds`, the review-surface stamp).

Two alternatives were rejected. **A discriminator column on `focus_session_tasks`** (an `off_plan` flag, inserting off-Plan rows there too) was rejected because the codebase encodes the invariant "the Plan *is* the set of `focus_session_tasks` rows" — every Plan reader (`watchActiveSessionTasks`, `watchSessionTasks`, the planning provider, the Plan half of `getReviewSurface`) would have to remember to filter the discriminator, and any single miss would silently auto-grow the Plan, violating ADR-0002 with a wide blast radius. The contract test `focus_session_review_contract_test.dart` (f) pins that invariant. **Full unification into one disposition table** (moving Plan Dispositions off `focus_session_tasks` too) was rejected as pure churn: it needs a non-destructive data migration of existing dispositions plus a rewrite of the shipped backend model and the `rollover`/`leave`/`maybe` contract groups, for cosmetic gain.

The dual-home partition keeps `focus_session_tasks` a pure Plan table (test (f) stays green permanently, no data migration), matches CONTEXT.md's definition of Disposition as a standalone `(FocusSession, Outcome)` relationship independent of Plan membership, and confines the new concept to callers that already reason about the Plan-vs-engaged union. The cost is that "the Disposition of an Outcome in a session" is a UNION of two tables rather than one column; the query helpers own that seam and the table docstrings cross-reference each other so a future reader is not surprised by two homes.
